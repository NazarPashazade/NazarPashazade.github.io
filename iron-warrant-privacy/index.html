IRON WARRANT — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This page is published at https://nazarpashazade.github.io/iron-warrant-privacy/

IRON WARRANT is a military shooter for Android and iOS, developed by Nazar Pashazade ("we"). You can play the whole campaign offline. Optional online team matches were added in version 1.0.0. This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

Playing offline sends nothing anywhere. If you choose online play and accept the online notice, the game uses Unity Gaming Services to connect you, and the other players in your match see your callsign and your soldier's actions. We have no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

  • your campaign and mission progress and results;
  • your soldier customisation and weapon loadout choices;
  • your settings (graphics quality, controls and touch layout, sound options, blood effects, reduce motion);
  • your choice of online callsign, your acceptance of the online notice, and the players you have muted or blocked.

This information stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Online play (optional)

Nothing is sent to the internet until you tap the online entry on the main menu and accept the online notice. When you host or join an online match:

  • Anonymous player ID. Unity Authentication gives your device a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email address or Google account, and the game asks for no account details. A shortened, hashed form of this ID is shared with the other players in your match so that muting and blocking keep working.
  • IP address. Unity's servers, and the other players' devices, see your IP address so that the connection can be made.
  • Match data. The other players in your match, and the player hosting it, receive your callsign or chosen name, your team, where your soldier is and how it moves, what it shoots, your hits, kills and deaths, and how your soldier looks.
  • Quick-chat and map pings. Preset callouts and pings you send are shown to the other players in your match. There is no free text chat. Voice chat is not part of this version.

The online services are provided by Unity Technologies. How Unity handles this data, including how long it keeps IP addresses, is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Deleting your online ID

You can delete your online ID in the game (the DELETE ONLINE ID button on the online screen) or by following the steps on our deletion page.

Reporting and blocking

In the lobby you can mute a player, block a player and report a player. Muting and blocking are stored on your device. Read our terms for the rules of online play.

Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts that you create, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. It does not read your contacts, location, files, microphone or camera, and it does not use advertising identifiers. It has no purchases.

Permissions

  • Internet access — used only for online play, after you accept the online notice.

The game asks for no other permissions.

Third parties

The game includes no third-party advertising or analytics software. Online play uses Unity Gaming Services (authentication, relay and sessions). If you install the game from Google Play or the Apple App Store, those stores process data about the download and about crashes under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information from them.

Age

IRON WARRANT is intended for players aged 18 and over because of its realistic military violence. Online play is for players aged 16 and over. The game is not directed at children.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
