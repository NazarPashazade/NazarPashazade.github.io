IRON WARRANT — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This page is published at https://nazarpashazade.github.io/iron-warrant-privacy/

IRON WARRANT is an offline single-player military shooter for Android and iOS, developed by Nazar Pashazade ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

IRON WARRANT does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so that it can continue where you left off:

  • your campaign and mission progress and results;
  • your soldier customisation and weapon loadout choices;
  • your settings (graphics quality, controls and touch layout, sound and voice options, blood effects, reduce motion).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. It does not read your contacts, location, files, microphone or camera, and it does not use advertising identifiers. It has no purchases and no online features.

Permissions

  • Internet access — declared by the game engine. The game itself makes no network requests and works fully offline.

The game asks for no other permissions.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising or analytics software. If you install it from Google Play or the Apple App Store, those stores process data about the download and about crashes under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information from them.

Age

IRON WARRANT is intended for players aged 18 and over. It contains realistic military violence and is not directed at children. The game does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
