Oak Puzzle: delete your account

Publisher: Atropia Studio. Updated: 23 November 2026.

An optional Oak Puzzle cloud account uses Firebase and your Google Play Games identity. You can delete it without deleting your Google Account.

Delete your cloud account in the app

  1. Open Oak Puzzle using the same Google Play Games account.
  2. Open Profile and choose "Delete cloud profile".
  3. Confirm deletion. If it fails, connect to the internet and retry.

Successful deletion removes your private cloud Profile, public nickname and avatar record, public Daily Challenge scores, and Firebase Authentication account.

After reinstalling, use the same Play Games account and the Profile cloud button to regain the deletion control.

Request deletion without the app

Email atropia.studio@gmail.com and request deletion of your Oak Puzzle cloud account and data. Include your Play Games player ID. Never send a password or sign-in token. We may ask you to verify ownership.

Remove public scores without deleting your account

Open the leaderboard and choose "Leave public leaderboard". This removes public scores and stops future submissions. Failed removal stays pending until you retry successfully.

If you separately enabled cloud Profile sync, your public nickname, built-in avatar and private Profile remain. Otherwise, the public record is deleted. Your Firebase account remains available for full deletion through Profile.

What stays and how long deletion takes

  • Your local Profile, game progress, coins, statistics and settings stay on your device.
  • Your Google Account, Play Games profile and earlier Google leaderboard scores are not deleted.
  • Uninstalling or clearing local app data does not delete your cloud account.
  • Firebase authentication IP logs remain for a few weeks.
  • Firebase live and backup authentication data can take up to 180 days to clear.

Read the Oak Puzzle privacy policy for the full data and retention details.
