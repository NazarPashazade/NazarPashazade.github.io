Oak Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This page is published at Oak Puzzle's privacy website.

Oak Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game published by ATROPIA ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

Oak Puzzle has no accounts and no analytics, and it keeps your progress and profile on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show them, Google may use your device's advertising ID. The Ads section explains this.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so that it can continue where you left off:

  • your best score;
  • the levels you have unlocked, the leaves and best score of each level, and the world you selected;
  • how many games you have played, how many lines you have cleared and your longest combo;
  • in Mahjong: the levels you have unlocked, and the stars, best time and best score of each level;
  • in Elixir Sort: the levels you have unlocked, the stars, best time and fewest moves of each level, the bottle style you chose, and an unfinished level so you can continue it;
  • in Wordora: the levels you have unlocked and the words you have found;
  • your coins in each game, which you earn by winning levels and spend on helpers;
  • your settings (language, sound, background music and its volume, theme, simple effects);
  • your profile, if you fill it in: the name you choose, your age, gender and country (all optional), the avatar you pick, and, if you choose one, a copy of the photo you use as your profile picture.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. The game opts out of Android's automatic backup, so it is not copied to your Google account either. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

To choose the language it starts in, the game reads your phone's language setting; this happens on the device and is not stored anywhere else. You can change the language in Settings.

Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts, no analytics and no tracking of our own. It does not read your contacts, location, files, microphone or camera. It does not guess your country from your location. We do not receive your advertising ID. Google AdMob uses it, as the Ads section says. It sees a photo only if you pick one yourself as your profile picture, through your phone's own photo picker: it then receives that one photo, keeps a copy on your device, and never sends it anywhere. No permission to your photo library is requested.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, in two ways:

  • Videos you choose to watch. You can watch a short video for extra coins, or to double the coins from a level.
  • Ads between levels. Now and then an ad appears after you finish a level. It never appears during play.

To show an ad, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and about how the ad performed. Google may use this to choose and measure ads. Google does this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information.

  • Your choice. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places, the game shows Google's consent message the first time it opens. Where you can change your answer, Settings shows "Ad privacy choices".
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google or Privacy. You can also turn off ad personalisation there.
  • Content. The game asks for ads suitable for a general audience.
  • On your device. The game saves only how many videos you have watched today and when ads last appeared, so that it can keep ads rare. This never leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Internet access — the game needs it to load ads and for the update check below. Without a connection, the game works, and no ads appear.
  • Advertising ID — lets Google AdMob show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Network state and wake lock — added by the ads software, so that it can check for a connection and play videos.
  • Change audio settings — lets the game play its sounds and music alongside other apps' audio instead of interrupting it.

The game asks for no other permissions. The links in the game (the full privacy policy and "Rate the game") open in your browser or in Google Play.

Updates

On Android, the game asks Google Play on your phone whether a newer version is available (Google Play's in-app updates). If one is, Google Play shows its own update dialog. Google Play handles the check and the download under its own privacy policy. The game sends nothing to us and learns only whether to offer the update. On your device it saves only the version you said no to and when, so that it does not ask again for a few days.

Third parties

The game includes one third-party service: Google AdMob, for ads (see Ads). It has no analytics software. If you install the game from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and about crashes under its own privacy policy; we receive only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from Google Play.

Age

Oak Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone. Ads are requested for a general audience.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
