Oak Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Reviewed for 3.4.2: 28 November 2026.

This page is published at Oak Puzzle's privacy website.

Oak Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game published by Atropia Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

Oak Puzzle has no analytics. Progress stays local, except Daily Challenge scores you explicitly choose to share. Profile details leave the device only through the optional features below. Android backup can keep a copy in your own Google Drive.

  • Google AdMob shows ads and may use your device's advertising ID.
  • Optional Firebase rankings publish your nickname, built-in avatar ID, player ID, Daily Challenge scores and their dates.
  • Optional Firebase friend invites store a random invite code with your Play Games player ID, readable only by you.
  • Optional Firebase cloud sync separately stores private Profile details and publishes your nickname and built-in avatar ID.
  • Optional purchases go through Google Play, which handles the payment. We receive no payment details.

The sections below explain these services and your choices.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so that it can continue where you left off:

  • your best score;
  • the levels you have unlocked, the leaves and best score of each level, and the world you selected;
  • how many games you have played, how many lines you have cleared and your longest combo;
  • in Mahjong: the levels you have unlocked, and the stars, best time and best score of each level;
  • in Elixir Sort: the levels you have unlocked, the stars, best time and fewest moves of each level, the bottle style you chose, and an unfinished level so you can continue it;
  • in Wordora: the levels you have unlocked and the words you have found;
  • your coins in each game, which you earn by playing and spend on helpers and stone sets, and how many coins each activity paid or cost (these totals stay on the device);
  • the stone sets you own, the awards you collected, the day's tasks and weekly goal you collected;
  • what you bought through Google Play: a record of each purchase (Google Play's purchase token) so that its coins are added once, and whether ads are removed;
  • your settings (language, sound, background music and its volume, theme, simple effects, the daily reminder and its hour);
  • the day's task progress, including Block Puzzle levels and which games you played, your streak and leaf shields;
  • the name and score of yesterday's leaderboard winner, so that the daily screen can show them;
  • your invite code, how many friends it has paid, and, for a friend, the code received from the install link and whether it was accepted;
  • your profile, if you fill it in: your chosen name, optional age, gender and country, and built-in avatar ID.

Only built-in avatars can be selected. Older custom-photo selections become the default avatar. Photo copies saved by older versions stay unused on the device until the app's local data is cleared. They are not uploaded.

We cannot see this local information, except details you choose to share or sync as described below. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes the local copy. It does not delete your cloud profile or account.

Android backup

If your phone's backup setting is on, Android copies this data to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Your progress then returns when you install the game again or set up a new phone.

Android does this, not us:

  • the copy belongs to your Google Account, and we cannot read it;
  • on Android 9 and later it is encrypted with your screen lock;
  • other apps cannot read it either;
  • it uses no space in your Google Drive;
  • only the newest copy is kept, and Android deletes it after a long time without use.

You can turn the backup off in your phone's settings, under System and then Backup.

To choose the language it starts in, the game reads your phone's language setting; this happens on the device and is not stored anywhere else. You can change the language in Settings.

Information we do not collect

The game has no analytics or tracking of our own. Optional cloud profiles use the Firebase account described below. Optional cloud features authenticate through Google Play Games; we do not ask for your e-mail address. The game does not read your contacts, location, files, microphone or camera. It does not guess your country from your location. We do not receive your advertising ID. Google AdMob uses it, as the Ads section says. The game does not open your photo library or offer custom profile photos.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, in two ways:

  • Videos you choose to watch. You can watch a short video for extra coins, to double the coins from a level, or for new pieces or an undo when you are stuck.
  • Ads between levels. Now and then an ad appears after you finish a level or an Endless or Daily game, before its result card. It never appears during play.

To show an ad, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and about how the ad performed. Google may use this to choose and measure ads. Google does this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information.

  • Your choice. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places, the game shows Google's consent message the first time it opens. Where you can change your answer, Settings shows "Ad privacy choices".
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google or Privacy. You can also turn off ad personalisation there.
  • Content. The game asks for ads suitable for a general audience.
  • On your device. The game saves only how many videos you have watched today and when ads last appeared, so that it can keep ads rare. This never leaves your device.

Leaderboards

Oak Puzzle's optional public rankings use Firebase, independently of Google's public gaming profile.

  • Your permission first. Tap "Join public leaderboard" to participate. Existing cloud-profile users are not enrolled automatically. All games remain available without joining.
  • Public details. Joining publishes your chosen nickname, built-in avatar ID, Play Games player ID, Daily Challenge scores and dates. Use a nickname without sensitive information. Your Google profile can remain private; Oak Puzzle does not change Google's privacy settings.
  • Scores. Only your saved first Daily Challenge result for today and future first results are submitted. Practice runs, ordinary levels and Endless scores are not submitted. Earlier Google scores are not imported.
  • Small records. One public record keeps your latest played local date, that day's score and your all-time best. We do not keep a full history of daily results.
  • Viewing. Anyone can read public ranking records without signing in. The game requests up to twenty records per query. Your own public result appears once in the list, or separately when outside the top twenty. Today follows the device's local date.
  • Leave. "Leave public leaderboard" removes your public scores and stops future submissions. Failed removal stays pending and retryable; scores may remain public until it succeeds. With separate cloud-profile consent, your public nickname, avatar ID and private Profile remain. Otherwise, the public record is deleted.
  • Separate consent. Joining does not enable private Profile sync or upload your age, gender or country. Leaving retains the Firebase account so Profile's deletion control remains available.
  • Earlier Google scores. Older versions submitted scores to Google Play Games. This version stops those submissions but does not delete existing Google scores. Manage those records through Google Play Games.

Optional cloud profiles

  • Your permission first. Profile → Sign in and sync profile enables Firebase cloud storage. Without this action, existing local Profile details are not uploaded.
  • Private details. Your chosen name, optional age, gender and country, and built-in avatar ID are stored privately. Only the matching authenticated player can read, change or delete that private profile.
  • Public details. Only your nickname, built-in avatar ID and Play Games player ID are publicly readable. Other players see changes when they reload their leaderboard. Cloud sync alone does not join the public ranking or publish scores.
  • Avatars only. Only a built-in avatar identifier is stored; avatar images already ship with the game. Firebase does not receive photos, other game progress, statistics, settings or local photo paths. Separately consented public Daily scores are described above.
  • Restore. Sign in through the Profile cloud button with the same Play Games account on another device. Existing cloud details restore automatically, without a separate restore step. Once enabled, changes sync automatically and the profile refreshes when the app returns to the foreground. Pending local edits take precedence over remote changes. Concurrent updates use the last successful write.
  • Authentication. Joining the ranking or enabling Profile sync creates a Firebase account through Play Games. Firebase Authentication verifies that identity. Firebase assigns an account ID and stores authentication state on your device. It processes sign-in information, IP addresses and user-agent information to operate the service and prevent abuse.
  • Storage. We manage the profile database in Cloud Firestore, in Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3). Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States. Connections use HTTPS. No Firebase Analytics is included. Firebase administrators can manage stored data; private details are not accessible to other players.
  • Public access. Public identities and consented scores are readable by anyone, including through bounded public queries. Private Profile records cannot be listed or read by other players.
  • Offline use. Profile edits remain local if sync fails. Profile shows a pending change or error and a retry button. Consented scores are saved locally for retry. A ranking read failure shows an error, not an empty-score success.
  • Delete the account. Profile → Delete cloud profile removes private details, public identity and scores, then the Firebase account. Your local Profile, progress and earlier Google scores stay. A failed deletion remains pending until retried. After reinstalling, use the same Play Games account and the cloud-profile button to regain the deletion control.
  • Deletion without the app. You can also request cloud-profile and account deletion at the contact address below. Include your Play Games player ID, but never a password or sign-in token. We may ask you to verify ownership.
  • Retention. We keep cloud profiles and accounts until you delete them or request deletion. Firebase keeps authentication IP logs for a few weeks. Its live and backup authentication data can take up to 180 days to clear.

See Firebase privacy and security information for Google's processing and retention details.

Permissions

  • Internet access — the game needs it to load ads, for the leaderboards, and for the update check below. Without a connection, the game works, and no ads appear.
  • Advertising ID — lets Google AdMob show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Network state and wake lock — added by the ads software, so that it can check for a connection and play videos.
  • Change audio settings — lets the game play its sounds and music alongside other apps' audio instead of interrupting it.
  • Billing — added by Google Play Billing, so that the game can offer its optional purchases.
  • Notifications — only if you turn the daily reminder on. Android also lets the game set its reminders again after the phone restarts.

The game asks for no other permissions. The links in the game (the full privacy policy and "Rate the game") open in your browser or in Google Play.

Invites and ratings

  • Invite friends (Android, signed in with Play Games) opens your phone's share sheet with a message and the game's Google Play link. The app you pick sends it; the game sends nothing.
    • Sharing creates a random 8-letter invite code. Firebase stores it with your Play Games player ID and the number of friends already paid. The link carries the code, not your name.
    • Only you can read your code. Other players cannot see it or who owns it.
    • A friend who installs from the link: the game reads Google Play's install referrer once and keeps the code on the device. After five won levels the friend may accept, signing in with Play Games.
    • Accepting stores the friend's player ID and the code in Firebase. It also adds an empty record to your code, so your game can count friends. The record holds no ID. Each account can accept once, never its own code.
    • Deleting the cloud profile removes your invite code and your acceptance. Empty join records stay and name no one.
  • Ratings. After a few won levels, the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Google Play shows it under its own policy, and we receive no information from it.

Purchases

The game sells optional coin packs and "Remove ads" through Google Play Billing. Google Play shows the price, takes the payment and keeps the record of the purchase under its own privacy policy. We receive no card or payment details.

On the device, the game keeps each purchase's token, so that the coins are added once, and whether ads are removed. On a new phone, "Restore purchases" asks Google Play again. Nothing about purchases is sent to us.

Everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

Reminders

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings, and you choose the hour. It is written by your own phone: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and the game needs no connection for it. At most one reminder arrives a day, and never at night. Turning the switch off removes every reminder the game had planned.

Updates

On Android, the game asks Google Play on your phone whether a newer version is available (Google Play's in-app updates). If one is, Google Play shows its own update dialog. Google Play handles the check and the download under its own privacy policy. The game sends nothing to us and learns only whether to offer the update. On your device it saves only the version you said no to and when, so that it does not ask again for a few days.

Third parties

The game uses Google AdMob for ads, Google Play Billing for optional purchases, and Play Games Services for optional cloud authentication. Firebase Authentication and Firestore support optional public rankings and cloud profiles. It has no analytics software. Android's own backup service, part of your phone, stores the copy described above under Google's privacy policy. If you install the game from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and about crashes under its own privacy policy; we receive only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from Google Play.

Age

Oak Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Choose a nickname rather than personal information when sharing a name. Ads are requested for a general audience.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
