Oak Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Oak Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game published by Nazar Pashazade ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

Oak Puzzle does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so that it can continue where you left off:

  • your best score;
  • the levels you have unlocked and the world you selected;
  • how many games you have played and how many lines you have cleared;
  • your settings (sound, ambience, vibration, simple effects).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Internet access — declared by the app framework. The game itself makes no network requests and works offline.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising or analytics software. If you install it from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and about crashes under its own privacy policy; we receive only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from Google Play.

Age

Oak Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
