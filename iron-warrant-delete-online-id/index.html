IRON WARRANT — Delete your online ID

Effective date: 23 November 2026

When you play online, Unity gives your device a random anonymous player ID. You can delete it at any time. Offline progress on your device is not affected.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open IRON WARRANT and go to the online screen.
  2. Tap DELETE ONLINE ID and confirm.

The game deletes the anonymous account at Unity, signs you out and clears your online data on the device. The next time you play online you get a new ID.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the game, email atropia.studio@gmail.com with the subject "IRON WARRANT online ID deletion". Uninstalling the game removes the ID from your device but not at Unity; to delete it there, use the in-game button or email us. Because the ID is anonymous, we may ask you to open the game and use the in-game button, since we cannot look up an ID by name.

What is not deleted

  • Your campaign progress, loadout and settings stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall.
  • Other players in a match you already played may remember your callsign; we cannot remove that from their memory.
  • Unity may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, for the period set in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Read the privacy policy for details.
